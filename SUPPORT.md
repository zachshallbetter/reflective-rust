# Support

Reflective Rust has a solo maintainer, so support is best-effort with no SLA.

- **Questions and bugs:** open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/zachshallbetter/reflective-rust/issues/new/choose).
  A concrete alternative or a minimal reproduction helps most.
- **Security reports:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately.
- **Versions:** layers version independently of one another and of release tags; the policy is
  [docs/meta/versioning.md](docs/meta/versioning.md) and changes are in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
