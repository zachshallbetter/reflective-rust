# AI and Semantic Tooling

**Status:** Research synthesis

Compiler-authored semantic graphs can provide AI systems with typed relationships, provenance, visibility, ownership, trait obligations, and generated/runtime correspondences that source-text retrieval alone cannot guarantee. Access should be capability-filtered and should preserve uncertainty where trait solving or normalization is ambiguous.
