# Future Work

**Status:** Research synthesis

Major research extensions:

- stable cross-crate semantic identity;
- reflection over higher-ranked lifetimes and generic associated types;
- trait-solver ambiguity representation;
- source provenance and macro-origin modeling;
- semantic diffing for incremental compilation;
- reflected ABI and FFI contracts;
- AI-safe semantic graph access;
- debugger and live-system integration;
- verified effect-handler domains;
- semantic spatial projection over compiler graphs.
