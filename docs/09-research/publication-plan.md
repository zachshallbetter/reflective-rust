# Publication Plan

**Status:** Research synthesis

Recommended publication sequence:

1. Position paper defining the taxonomy and staged architecture.
2. Language-design paper for semantic reflection and compile-time execution.
3. Systems paper on runtime projection and safe dynamic invocation.
4. Compiler paper on dependency tracking, staging, and incremental compilation.
5. Research paper on explicit procedural-reflection domains.
6. Companion paper on the Compiler Semantic Graph and semantic interfaces for AI and SSP.

Each paper should preserve the observation/inference/proposal distinction required by the research priming document.
