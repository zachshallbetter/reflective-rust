# Academic Bibliography & Citations

The research program of **Reflective Rust** draws upon and extends the following canonical publications in programming language theory, compiler design, and reflective architectures:

---

## Academic References

1. **Smith, B. C. (1982)**. *Procedural Reflection in Programming Languages*. Doctoral dissertation, Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Cambridge, MA.
2. **Kiczales, G., des Rivières, J., & Bobrow, D. G. (1991)**. *The Art of the Metaobject Protocol*. MIT Press, Cambridge, MA.
3. **Matsakis, N. D., & Klock II, F. S. (2014)**. *The Rust Language*. ACM SIGAda Ada Letters, 34(3), 103-104.
4. **Vandersvoorde, D., Josuttis, N. M., & Gregor, D. (2017)**. *C++ Templates: The Complete Guide (2nd Edition)*. Addison-Wesley Professional.
5. **Revzin, B., et al. (2025)**. *P2996R13: Reflection for C++26*. WG21 paper, 20 June 2025. https://www.open-std.org/jtc1/sc22/wg21/docs/papers/2025/p2996r13.html
6. **Jung, R., Jourdan, J. H., Krebbers, R., & Dreyer, D. (2017)**. *RustBelt: Securing the Foundations of the Rust Programming Language*. Proceedings of the ACM on Programming Languages, 2(POPL), 1-34.
7. **Bezanson, J., Edelman, A., Karpinski, S., & Shah, V. B. (2017)**. *Julia: A Fresh Approach to Numerical Computing*. SIAM Review, 59(1), 65-98.
8. **Goldberg, A., & Robson, D. (1983)**. *Smalltalk-80: The Language and Its Implementation*. Addison-Wesley.
