# D Zig Nim

**Status:** Research synthesis

D, Zig, and Nim show alternative compile-time models. D offers broad traits and compile-time function execution; Zig exposes type information during comptime; Nim combines AST macros and semantic typing. Their strongest lesson is that compile-time expressiveness must be paired with predictable staging and diagnostics.
