# Safepoints and Capabilities

**Status:** Research synthesis

Every process mutation should be authorized by a capability such as inspect, replace-local, install-handler, resume, abort, or redirect-within-certified-targets. Capabilities must be unforgeable and scoped to one reflected process instance.
