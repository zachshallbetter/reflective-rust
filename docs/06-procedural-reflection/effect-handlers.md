# Effect Handlers

**Status:** Research synthesis

Algebraic-effect-style handlers provide a disciplined form of execution intercession. Rather than exposing arbitrary continuation mutation, a reflectable domain may surface declared effects and typed resumptions. This is a research direction, not an established Rust design.
